Routine or deep clean?

Here's what each usually includes. Your quote spells out the exact scope for your property.

Routine cleaning

Recurring visits that keep a property in shape

  • Dusting reachable surfaces and furniture
  • Vacuuming, sweeping, and mopping floors
  • Cleaning and disinfecting restrooms
  • Wiping kitchen counters, sinks, and appliance exteriors
  • Emptying trash and recycling
  • Spot-cleaning glass and entry doors

Deep cleaning

Everything in routine cleaning, plus

  • Baseboards, door frames, and trim
  • Vents, light fixtures, and ceiling fans
  • Inside microwaves and other appliances
  • Scrubbing grout, tile, and built-up grime
  • Interior windows, sills, and tracks
  • Cabinet fronts and hard-to-reach areas

Questions

How do you price your services?

Every job is quoted individually. Pricing depends on square footage, the type of property, the scope of work, and how often you need service. Request a quote and we'll put together a clear, written proposal.

Do you only work on contract?

Most of our clients use us on a recurring contract, which is the best way to keep a property consistently clean. We also quote one-time deep cleans, move-outs, and post-construction cleanups.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Windy City Property Services is licensed and insured.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Chicago's northwest suburbs and serve the city of Chicago and suburbs throughout Chicagoland. If you're not sure we reach your property, just ask.

Can you clean outside business hours?

Yes. We schedule around your operations, including early mornings, evenings, and weekends, so cleaning doesn't get in the way of your tenants or staff.

What cleaning products do you use?

We favor non-toxic, eco-conscious products. If your building has specific product requirements, tell us and we'll work with them.
